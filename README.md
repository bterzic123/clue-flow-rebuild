# Growth Audit — Clue

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/clue-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Clue already has. It comes with a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall, rebuilt in Clue's own design. Only the order of the screens changes.

Prepared by Adapty, September 2026, from the live iOS flow, the App Store listing and helloclue.com.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Ask for the account after the paywall | Sign-up | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 02 | Show the next-period date before the price | Payoff | CR +10–15% |
| 03 | Open the paywall on their mode and your 4.8 | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Move permissions and consent toggles past the paywall | Reminder | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | A second chance on close, at your $29.99 price | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |

Run together, the five could lift CR to purchase by up to **+25%** and ARPU by up to **+30%**.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from Clue's own published material:

- **App Store listing:** 4.8 from 405K ratings, and the in-app purchases at $39.99, $9.99 and $29.99
- **helloclue.com:** 100M+ downloads, the 25% off Clue Plus web offer, the Free vs Clue Plus plan comparison and the verified-user quotes
- **Clue's in-app screens:** the paywall review and the 7-day trial copy

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Clue.
