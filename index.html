What this is

We opened Clue the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Clue: installed it, went through all 27 screens from welcome to purchase, read your App Store listing and helloclue.com, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people before the price.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: the cycle ring, the five mode cards, the calendar, the wheel with its pink bar, the plum trial card, the teal buttons. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already have but do not show at the right moment. This is not a design pitch.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
